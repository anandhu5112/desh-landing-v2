## 5. FAQ (`FaqSection`)

H2: **"Frequently Asked Questions"** — accordion, one open at a time.

1. **Who can invest through Desh?**
   NRIs, OCIs, and PIOs holding a valid passport can invest with Desh. We support investors across the US, UK, UAE, Singapore, Australia, and most other jurisdictions.
2. **Do I need to be in India to start investing?**
   No. You can complete the entire onboarding process remotely and manage your investments from anywhere in the world.
3. **What investment options do you offer?**
   Indian mutual funds and US stocks, alongside curated portfolios built around your goals, time horizon, and risk appetite.
4. **Will I get a dedicated advisor?**
   Yes. Every investor is paired with a dedicated advisor you can reach whenever you need guidance — not a rotating support queue.
5. **Can I start a SIP from overseas?**
   Yes. You can set up and manage a SIP entirely online from your NRE or NRO account, with contributions debited automatically each month.
6. **Is my money held by Desh?**
   No. Your money never sits with us — investments are held directly with SEBI-regulated fund houses and custodians in your own name.

**Closing tagline (below the accordion):**
"A modern investing experience built for NRIs who want a clean, credible path into Indian mutual funds & US stocks without getting buried in paperwork, confusion, or bad advice."

## Content inventory summary

**Scroll order:** Nav → Hero (pinned, 2 beats) → US → Advisor → India → SIP calculator → Portfolio CTA → WhatsApp card → FAQ → Footer.

**Value proposition ladder:** global-citizen identity → distance is not a barrier → currency hedge (US) → human advice → beat FDs (India) → project your own outcome → join the community → objection handling → close.

**CTA distribution:** "Talk to an Advisor" ×4 · "Book a Free Consultation" ×1 · "Join Our Community" ×2 · "Contact us" (nav) ×1 · "Send message" (modal) ×1.

**Every claim made on the page:**
- ₹1 Crore+ assets guided
- 100+ happy NRI investors / 999+ active NRI investors *(two different community numbers)*
- 100% personalized guidance
- 24–48 hr response time
- "Always" human support / dedicated (non-rotating) advisor
- 12% assumed annual return (disclaimed as illustrative)
- Money held with SEBI-regulated fund houses in the investor's own name
- Eligibility: NRI / OCI / PIO with valid passport; US, UK, UAE, Singapore, Australia "and most other jurisdictions"
- Fully remote onboarding; SIP from NRE/NRO accounts
- Company logos shown as a "trusted by" row (Apple, Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, Accenture, FedEx, Walmart)

## Open questions worth vetting

1. **Placeholder metadata still shipping** — title/description say "Create Next App".
2. **Inconsistent social proof** — "100+ Happy NRI Investors" (Contact tab) vs "999+ Active NRI Investors" / "+999" (Join tab). Which is the real number, and are these substantiated?
3. **Company logo row** — Apple/Google/Meta etc. presented under a "trusted by" framing. In the code comment these are meant to read as *employers of members*, but nothing on screen says that. Reads as a client/partner endorsement.
4. **Decorative portfolio card** — "₹ 24,72,480 · +12.84% · updated 2 mins ago" looks like live product data. It is invented.
5. **Regulatory footing** — the page makes investment claims and shows a return projection with no visible entity name, registration number, jurisdiction disclosure, risk disclaimer beyond the calculator note, privacy policy, or terms.
6. **12% assumed return** — fixed and not user-adjustable; the only disclaimer is beneath the calculator.
7. **No functional conversion path** — every CTA either opens a modal that doesn't submit anywhere, or does nothing. No email, phone, WhatsApp deep link, or booking link.
8. **Copyright reads © 2026** with no company legal name.
9. **Section order** — US (currency hedge) leads before India (mutual funds), while the hero and the closing tagline both lead with India. Intentional?
10. **CTA repetition** — "Talk to an Advisor" four times with identical wording; the one variant ("Book a Free Consultation") is arguably the strongest offer and appears only once.
